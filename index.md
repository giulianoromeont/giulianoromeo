## About me

Since October 2026: I am a postdoc at [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr) within the ERC Synergy Grant “[DynAMiCs](https://dynamics.mpi-sws.org)”, working at [IRIF](https://www.irif.fr) in Paris.

<br>
Before that:
- I was a research fellow at [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it), within the project "Cryptographic models and protocols for the tokenization of real-world assets with physical collateral".
- I was a research fellow at the [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/it), within the PRIN 2022 project "Algebraic methods in Cryptanalysis".
- I was a postdoc at [Charles University](https://www.mff.cuni.cz) in Prague, within the PRIMUS/25/SCI/008 project "Automorphic forms for symplectic groups via trace formula".
- I was a postdoc at [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it), within the PNRR M4C2 MICS project "Post-quantum cryptographic solutions for blockchain security".
- I received my Ph. D. at [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it), defending a thesis from the title "[Advances in the theory of $p$-adic continued fractions](https://iris.polito.it/retrieve/e40f3b18-f536-473a-bfa9-3531707180df/conv_romeo_tesi.pdf)"

<br>
Some of my research interests from **number theory** and **cryptography** are:

- continued fractions and generalizations;
- Diophantine approximation and transcendence;
- quadratic forms over number fields;
- post-quantum cryptography and blockchain.
